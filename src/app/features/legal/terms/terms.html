What the pages are

The service and country pages describe pathways we prepare: work, study, family, residence, visit, and related permits. They are general. They are not a legal opinion on your case, and they are not a promise that a visa, permit, or residence will be granted.

The assessment

Sending the form asks for a reply. It does not hire us. A paid engagement starts only after you accept a written plan that names the pathway, the documents, the timing, and the fee.

You agree that the name, contact details, and facts you send are yours to share and are accurate as far as you know. A file built on wrong facts can be refused, and we can stop work if that comes to light.

Decisions we do not control

Embassies, immigration departments, employers, and schools make their own decisions. Processing times and rules change. We prepare the file and follow it up. We do not control the outcome, and a fee for preparation is not a fee for an approval.
